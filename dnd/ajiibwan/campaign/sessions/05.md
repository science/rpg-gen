## What happened

- **The ants (9b) — solved, not fought.** The party worked out how to re-freeze the Giant Ants.
  **Jiyloo held the door — "hodor'd" them back — while the party ran to 9a's pools and washed
  their feet, then washed hers.** The ants went still. This is the designed exit
  (*Arrive clean*), played straight.
- **The oath.** **Everyone except Tath'Shar** knelt in one of 9a's four doorframes and took
  Doorne into their heart. **Tath'Shar declined and is *Unwelcome*** (−1d4, but only against
  Doorne's own work).
- **An old-school full crawl, almost no fighting.** The party learned the architecture grammar
  (clean pyramid, sealed, washing, no passage, the open mouth), read lintels before touching
  doors, and routed around the marked cells. **Route: west, then south, then north — past 9o
  and the treasure wing — into the great hall (9c), and back to the secret-door area at its
  north end.**
- **They slammed 9f shut on An-Zefful.** Opened the defaced door, saw the minotaur, closed it.
  He is alive and still in his room.
- **The new statue.** They found the petrified Black Water cultist in a corridor and took what he
  had: a **black obsidian serpent ring** — the cult's membership token, useful for passing as
  one of them.
- **No other loot found or taken.** They walked through the cult camp in 9c without searching it;
  its papers (tally, dispatch, letter home, mapcase) are still there.
- **Ended at the hidden door between the two armoured statues** at the north end of 9c — the way
  into 9o (the false treasure room), and beyond it 9p.

## Decided after the session (GM)

- **The wight in 9p is not Sia.** It is **the Keeper — Kamenhet, Sia's high priest**, who made
  himself a wight when the empire fell so the oasis would stay safe for the king's temples and
  travellers, and who sealed himself into the inner tomb because of the evil that becoming a wight
  put in him. Rational and reasonable, but dangerous and unstable. Sia is dead and at rest in the
  sarcophagus. Full write-up: `scenarios/ashes-at-the-sphinx.md` → 9p card and *Running the Keeper*.
