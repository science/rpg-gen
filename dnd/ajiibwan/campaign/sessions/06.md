# Session 6 — The Priest Who Stayed
*Date: 2026-10-24*

**Party (5 PCs):** Ren (Toan, she/her, Halfling) · Orkideh (Steph, she/her, Elf) · Lia (Ania,
she/her, Tiefling) · Gereon (Mia, he/him, Dwarf Monk, Acolyte background) · Tath'Shar (Phoenix,
they/them, Drow Rogue — **Phoenix was absent; Steve ran Tath'Shar as an NPC with input from the
players present**).
**Companion:** Jiyloo (she/her), **now a full member of the party** (see below). **Animal
companion:** Orkideh's dog.

## What happened

- **The stranded cultist (9o).** The party found the cultist stranded on 9o's ledge (Tabbat, in
  the packet), out of water, and rescued him. They got information out of him.
- **The Keeper.** In 9p the party met the Dornian wight, Kamenhet, and he negotiated with them.
  His ask: **relight the fires in the temple.**
- **The Keeper's charge to Ren.** The Black Water Cult cannot get into Sia, so it has **retreated
  to a pyramid in the desert as its eastern outpost.** The Keeper asked Ren to investigate it, and
  gave her a religious and magical **blessing** to find that Dornian pyramid and try to protect it
  from the corruption the cult brought to Sia.
- **The cultist left with the Keeper.** At the end of the conversation, as the party was leaving,
  the Keeper asked them to **leave the cultist inside his inner tomb.** Why is unclear; it is
  ominous. They did. **Jiyloo left him a lot of food and water — "so he doesn't die quickly."**
  She is chaotic good, but she wanted vengeance for the murders above and the desecration below.
- **Nothing else** happened in 9o or 9p.
- **The Ember is relit.** The party relit the temple flame and protected the oasis from
  wrong-doers. The cult cannot get back in.
- **Ren can see the way north.** A faint, possibly magical path leads north from the oasis into
  the desert. There are several routes to the pyramid, and the blessing makes it easier for Ren
  to find them.
- **Tel A'bib — the deal with Elmingwed.** Tath'Shar (NPC'd) did much of the talking, with
  Elmingwed and with the merchants. The party negotiated to **take public credit with the
  merchants** for saving the oasis, instead of being paid as Elmingwed's contractors. He threw a
  **banquet** that raised their status and celebrity, and **passed a collection plate** among the
  merchants to fund their payment. He delivered his end of the bargain.
- **Money and gear.** After selling their old equipment, the party has **52 gp**. Elmingwed gave
  **studded leather armour to Ren and Lia.**
- **Jiyloo joins.** The party hired Jiyloo as a **full member of the party, for 1/5 of the
  party's loot.** She is no longer Elmingwed's hired guide.
- **The road to Durgan's Rest.** On the second day, climbing the trail into the mountains below
  Durgan's Rest and before they made camp, they were surprised by a **friendly troupe of kobolds
  with a wagon of junk to trade.**

**The game ended there**, with the kobolds' wagon on the trail in front of them.

## Settled after the session (GM)

- **Where the pyramid is.** Tel A'bib is on the eastern edge of the desert, a bit north of centre.
  The pyramid is due west of Tel A'bib and due north of the Oasis of Sia, in the deeper desert
  about 20 miles from any working road. That's why the cult calls it its *eastern* outpost.
- **The language engine** is owed by the priests at Sia **if the pyramid is cleared.**
- **The captive monk** — the female cult monk Gereon ran down in session 4 — **went to
  Elmingwed.** Ominous, and it's not yet clear what use he has for her. She may return as a
  de-programmed Elmingwed employee: hard cult turned hard merchant, with her loyalties switched
  and still unstable (`thread:the-turned-monk`).
