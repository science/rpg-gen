## What happened

### Tel A'bib — Elmingwed's compound
- **Jeffrol's disposition resolved.** Negotiation with [[npc:elmingwed]] ends with Jeffrol
  put to work rather than handed over or killed: he is dispatched up the **northern trade
  road toward the Granite Empire** to personally shepherd the camel venture his stolen
  operating money is riding on, and see that Elmingwed's share actually pays out. He is
  **slated to return in 9 weeks.** This is `scenarios/elmingweds-web.md` Option B's Jeff
  half — delivered, then spent — without the party taking Option B's mission.
- **GM subplot planted (not yet in play):** on that northern road Jeffrol will ingratiate
  himself with the **m'Thorsz at [[loc:hothme]]** and set himself up as a trading partner —
  and, in time, a thorn. He resurfaces as a rival operator if/when the party reaches Hothme
  and the western edge of the Vanic Imperial desert. See `thread:jeffrol-hothme`.
- **The letter to Tsendur.** Elmingwed's sealed letter for [[npc:tsendur]] at
  [[loc:durgans-rest]] is now in the party's hands. **They debated carrying it and did not.**
  The party knows only that they are asked to deliver it; they have not read it and do not
  know it warns Tsendur that a drow wizard is hunting prohibited artifacts.
  Tath'Shar knows privately that the wizard is their sister **Maghiel**, and that she is
  operating in the region — which is why they are here at all. The crux is still ahead:
  delivering the letter is plausibly the act that puts Maghiel on Durgan's Rest's map.
- **The fork, and how the table resolved it.** Gold or reputation. Take Elmingwed's fee for
  carrying the message to Tsendur, or take no fee and instead put Elmingwed *and* the
  merchants of Tel A'bib in the party's debt by reopening the choked trade route at the
  [[loc:oasis-of-sia]]. **The party chose reputation.** Tath'Shar argued for Tsendur
  (holding their private reason back) but accepted the decision without a fight.
  → Elmingwed's Option A; `scenarios/ashes-at-the-sphinx.md` is live.

### The Oasis of Sia — the ruined town
- Party + Jiyloo arrive. They work the **burnt tents** and find the carnage as written:
  bodies, wreckage, nothing living.
- **The dog (area 4).** Trapped under a collapsed tent, starving. Orkideh passes Animal
  Handling, casts *Speak with Animals*, and befriends it; **Gereon heals it with magic**,
  which seals the loyalty. **The dog is now Orkideh's**, a permanent companion.
  - *Standing benefit:* passive Perception (scent) for the party.
  - *Potential:* with training, active Perception (scent) checks; or Orkideh spends a
    *Speak with Animals* to brief it on what to look for and hear back what it smells.
  - ⚠️ **Open — and the source is more interesting than it first looks.** This is the
    **El-Alouph** family's dog, from the wagon-and-camel shop (area 4). That family
    **surrendered and was taken as slaves, led off into the desert** — so the dog is not a
    lead on a hiding place, it is a **lead on the slave caravan** ("unable to find its family,
    it will follow the party, or lead them on the trail to its family").
  - ⚠️ **Separately open:** the survivors in the covered hole near the pond are a *different*
    household — **Afara Al-Doorne and her daughter Dasara**, from the Elven Goods shop (area 5).
    Abd Al-Doorne was caught and killed getting them hidden. They are alive, starving, and
    **the party has not found them.** The best scene in the module is still sitting there
    unplayed.
- **The tavern (area 3).** Massive slaughter of locals. Scraping noises upstairs resolve
  into **4 zombies locked in a room** — raised by the cult and corralled there against
  future need, not left as a trap. The party killed all four **in a single round** with
  clean tactics.

### The sphinx — the guards
- Three Black Water cultists were **lounging in the shade of the sphinx's plinth** instead
  of standing watch. Neither side surprised the other.
- This is the cult's running characterization, now confirmed at the table: **the lower ranks
  are dullards and lackwits.** The institution is another matter — mean-spirited, vindictive,
  long-memoried, and capable well beyond its individual members. Seniors hold real positions
  of power and influence, run blackmail and behind-the-scenes control, and the clerics have
  magic that genuinely hurts. Their reliquaries hold real artifacts. *The Scientology of
  the Desert.*
- The party subdued all three without much trouble. The **leading monk (female)** broke for
  the open desert and was **run down by Gereon**, who matched her speed. **One cultist
  killed, two captured.**
- **Interrogation.** The captives gave up the timeline: the **main body of cultists left
  about a week ago** to obtain **magical items needed to penetrate the tomb** — the treasure
  the cult came for is behind something they could not get through. They are **expected back
  within 1-2 days.**
- The captives then began blaming each other for who had watch. The party had no patience
  for it.
- **The party executed one of the prisoners.** Tath'Shar was glad to do it; the less
  bloody-minded characters found something to look at over by the sphinx while it happened.
  **This is a real, recorded act — a bound prisoner killed on the threshold of a god's tomb.**
  No one outside the party saw it.
- The surviving leader was made to **show the party the entrance between the sphinx's paws**
  and to tell what she knew of what lies below — which was little; she was never part of the
  descent.

### Into the tomb
- **Jiyloo led down the stair.** The moment she saw 9a — the pools, the four statues, the
  gold-flecked black marble — she pulled a **Doornian pyramid pendant out from under her
  robes**, wore it openly, and said: *"This area is holy for believers of Doorne."*
  **That is why the Caryatid Columns did not attack.** She announced it in front of everyone;
  the party has the fact, whether or not anyone has drawn the conclusion from it yet.
- Party then enters **9a, the Hall of Water** (the pool hall, four statues, gold-flecked black
  marble) and goes **east**.
- The long hallway ends in **two doors: one south, one east.** They open the **east** door
  and are attacked by **Giant Ants (9b)**.
- They slam the door. The ants **hammer it and force it back open.**
- **Session ends here** — party in a narrow hallway, door open, ants coming.

## GM notes carried forward (Steve's own, verbatim intent)
- **The ants are not a mandatory fight.** If the party can improvise a way to wedge, spike,
  or brace the door shut, let it work. Fighting is equally fine.
- **Reward the tactical read.** Backing up into 9a and making the ants come through the
  doorway one at a time is the correct play and should visibly pay off.
- **Standing in the corridor to fight is the lesson.** A party that chooses to brawl in a
  narrow hall gets an honest demonstration of 5e positioning — flanked, no room to withdraw,
  casters in the line.
